COMPANY LEAVE POLICY (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

1. Annual Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave may be carried
forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the following calendar year. Any leave beyond
this cap will lapse on December 31st.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward
and resets each calendar year.

3. Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for up to two children,
in accordance with local labor law. Maternity leave can begin up to 8 weeks before the
expected delivery date.

4. Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be availed within
3 months of the child's birth.

5. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of
an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).

6. Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval and
role suitability. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off.

7. Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in
advance, except in emergencies. Sick leave may be applied for retroactively within
2 working days of return.
